Advisory

Find where your firm loses time and margin. Fix the biggest leak first.

We help owner-run firms put AI and data to work. It starts with a free 25-minute call. If there is something worth looking into, we run a fixed-fee diagnostic and tell you what to fix first. The fix itself is optional; you can do it with us or on your own.

How it works

Three steps. You can stop after any of them.

1

Free 25-minute call

We call it a capacity review. You tell us about one process that eats time or one number you don't trust. We tell you honestly whether a diagnostic is worth doing. No cost, no obligation.

2

Fixed-fee diagnostic

We look at how the work really gets done, using your own files and tools. The fee is agreed before we start and doesn't grow by the hour. We quote after the call, because it depends on the size of the firm.

3

Optional build

If you want, we build the fix: a workflow, an automation or a small tool. Or you take the plan to your own team or another vendor. Your choice.

The diagnostic

Where time and margin usually leak

We look at the everyday work, not just the software. These are the five places we check first.

Manual re-keying

Typing the same data from one place into another: invoices, bank statements, order exports, client documents.

Reconciliation

Matching books against the bank, GST returns, payment gateways or marketplace payouts by hand.

Reporting and data

Rebuilding the same monthly report in spreadsheets, and numbers that don't agree from one tool to the next.

Pricing

Discounts, fees and product margins that nobody has checked against what the work or the product really costs.

Follow-ups

Chasing documents, payments, approvals and repeat customers by phone and memory.

What you get

A short written report and a plan

Findings, ranked

Each finding says what is happening, roughly what it costs you, and how hard it is to fix. The list is ranked by value and effort.

A plan

What to fix first, what each fix needs (tools, data, people), and what can wait.

A straight answer

If an existing tool or a small change solves the problem, the report says so. You don't have to buy anything from us.

The method

VALUE OS: five steps

Every diagnostic follows the same five steps, so you always know where we are.

  1. Step 1

    Baseline

    Write down how the work is done today and how long it takes, from your real files and a few short conversations with the team.

  2. Step 2

    Diagnose

    Find where the time and money go, and check each suspicion against the records before calling it a finding.

  3. Step 3

    Quantify

    Put a rough number on each finding, in hours a month or rupees a year, so the findings can be compared.

  4. Step 4

    Design

    Rank the fixes by value and effort, and set out what to do first.

  5. Step 5

    Drive

    If you go ahead, build the fix and check against the baseline that it really helped.
