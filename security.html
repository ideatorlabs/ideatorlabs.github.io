Security

Security and confidentiality

Last updated 17 Nov 2026

Here is exactly what we commit to whenever we work with your business data. Plain promises, not badges.

1. What we commit to in client work

  • An NDA on request. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share anything, if you ask for one.
  • The least data needed. We ask only for the data the job needs. Where a sample or masked data is enough, we use that.
  • Your systems, or read-only copies. Where possible we work inside your own systems, or on read-only exports, rather than taking copies of live data.
  • No training on your data. We never use client data to train public AI models.
  • Deletion at the end. At the end of an engagement, we delete our working copies of your data when you ask.

2. This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages and served over HTTPS. It has no logins and no accounts, and it doesn’t store anything you type. The contact form opens your own email app. See our Privacy policy for details.

3. Report a security issue

If you find a security problem with this website or anything we have built, please email ideatorlabs.ai@gmail.com with “Security” in the subject line. We will reply and keep you updated until it is fixed.
