## 0. Before you start — the account setup (do this right once)

- Create the page **from your own personal LinkedIn** (LinkedIn requires a personal admin — there's no standalone company login). The admin link is **private**; visitors never see who runs the page.
- To keep it un-linked from your public identity: **do not add "Ideator Labs" to your personal Experience** unless/until you want the association. That's the only thing that creates a visible link.
- **Do not** make a second/fake personal account — it violates LinkedIn's terms and a ban would take the page down with it.
- You can add a second admin later (a co-founder, or a burner-free real account of someone you trust) from Admin tools → Manage admins.

**Create:** LinkedIn → top-right **For Business** (grid icon) → **Create a Company Page** → **Company** → fill the fields below.

# 6. Thought-Leadership Kit — look like a top firm from day one

Top firms read as authoritative because of **specificity + a named format + a real point of view** — not volume. Three moves:

## A. The signature series — "The Leak Report"

A recurring, branded format. Each edition takes ONE operational leak, quantifies it, and shows how to find it in your own business. This is your franchise — the thing people follow the page *for*. It reads as an established research machine even in week one, and it's endlessly fuelled by the blueprint's Field Findings + problem catalog.

**Format (repeat every edition):**
> 📊 THE LEAK REPORT · No. [N]
> [The leak, in one line]
>
> THE PATTERN — [1–2 sentences, specific]
> THE NUMBER — [the quantified impact]
> HOW TO SEE IT — [3–4 concrete steps a reader can run themselves]
> THE FIX — [1 line on what closes it]
>
> Most businesses never measure this. The ones that do find [X]. — ideatorlabs.github.io

**First 6 editions (one per Field Finding — all ready to schedule):**
1. **Customer profitability** — 15–30% of customers are unprofitable and invisible. See it: revenue − fully-loaded cost, per customer, ranked.
2. **Pricing drift** — one point of price = 8–12% of profit, yet most SMBs haven't repriced in 18+ months. See it: list price vs. realized price, by segment.
3. **Manual back-office** — 20–40% of admin payroll is work AI now does. See it: a one-week task log across the team, tagged rule-based vs. judgment.
4. **Cash-conversion drag** — $500k–$5M sits trapped in the cycle. See it: DSO + inventory days − payables days.
5. **Customer-service load** — 30–60% of contacts are repeat questions. See it: tag one week of tickets by root cause; Pareto them.
6. **Shelfware** — most teams use ~20% of the ERP/CRM they pay for. See it: license utilization + count of shadow spreadsheets.

## B. Insight posts (between Leak Reports — the "POV" voice)

- "The most expensive number in your business is the one nobody owns. Here's how to find who owns margin." 
- "AI won't fix a broken process — it'll just run it faster. Sequence matters: measure → fix → automate."
- "We don't sell transformation. We sell a number, proven in 3 weeks. Here's why fixed-scope beats open-ended."
- "Every 'we'll do it internally' project we've seen was still on the roadmap a year later. Certainty is the product."

## C. Who the Page should follow & engage (as the Page, never personal)

Following shapes your admin feed and, when you comment *as the page*, puts you in credible rooms. Curate ~20:

- **Strategy / consulting POV:** Harvard Business Review, McKinsey, BCG, Bain, MIT Sloan Management Review, First Round Review
- **AI frontier:** Anthropic, OpenAI, Hugging Face, a16z, Sequoia
- **Data/eng:** dbt Labs, Snowflake, Databricks
- **India ecosystem (your Track-A clients live here):** NASSCOM, Inc42, YourStory, TiE, ICAI (for the CA beachhead), relevant D2C/founder communities
- **Engage rule:** 2–3 thoughtful comments/week *as the Page* on these firms' posts — a specific, additive comment on an HBR or a16z post gets your page seen by exactly the right audience. Never generic praise; always add a data point or a sharper framing.

## D. The "top firm" rules (what to never do)

- ❌ No motivational-poster posts, no emoji spam, no "excited to announce" fluff.
- ✅ Every post either teaches a specific thing or states a defensible opinion.
- ✅ Always a number, a framework, or a step-by-step — never vague.
- ✅ Consistency > frequency. One sharp Leak Report/week beats five thin posts.
- ✅ Same voice everywhere: measured, specific, quietly confident. "The numbers have to defend themselves."
