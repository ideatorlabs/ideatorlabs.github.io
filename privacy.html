Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 July 2026

Ideator Labs ("we", "us") is a management & technology consulting studio. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to talk to you and do good work. This page explains what we hold and why.

What we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, email, company, and anything you share when you contact us or during an engagement.
  • This website collects almost nothing. The site is static. The interactive value-at-stake model runs entirely in your browser — the numbers you enter are never transmitted to us or stored anywhere.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard server request data (e.g. IP, user agent) as any web host does. We do not add our own trackers or advertising cookies.

How we use it

Only to respond to you, scope and deliver work, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data, and we never use one client's data to serve another or to train models.

Sharing

We share data only with the minimal service providers needed to run an engagement (e.g. email, cloud storage), each bound by confidentiality — never with advertisers.

Retention & your rights

We keep information only as long as the relationship and law require, then delete or return it. You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold — just email us.

Security

How we protect data during engagements is described on our Security & Confidentiality page.

This page is a plain-language summary provided in good faith, not legal advice. For a regulated engagement, have your own counsel review the terms that will govern our work.
