Privacy policy

Last updated 17 Nov 2026

Ideator Labs is a product studio and AI and data advisory. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you visit this website or write to us.

1. This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ad trackers. This is a plain static website. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or other third-party scripts.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes (GitHub Pages documentation). We don’t receive or use those logs. GitHub’s handling is covered by the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Site search. The search box runs inside your browser. What you type is not sent anywhere.

2. The contact form and email

  • The contact form doesn’t send anything by itself. It opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us. Nothing reaches us until you press send in your email app.
  • Where your email goes. Emails go to ideatorlabs.ai@gmail.com, a Gmail account, so they are stored by Google.
  • What we use it for. Only to reply to you and, if you ask, to discuss or do work for you. We don’t sell or share your details, and we don’t add you to a mailing list.

3. Client work

If we work together, the data you share is covered by our written agreement, and by an NDA if you ask for one. We never use client data to train public AI models. Our Security page lists what we commit to.

4. How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as the conversation, and any work that follows, needs them. Ask us to delete them and we will, unless the law requires us to keep something (for example, an invoice).

5. Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email ideatorlabs.ai@gmail.com and we will reply.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
