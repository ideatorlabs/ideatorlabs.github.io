Showrooms and stores

Retail

FurniTrailAdvisory

What gets in the way

  • • Visitors leave to think it over, and many are never heard from again.
  • • Follow-up happens on staff phones, when someone remembers.
  • • Nobody can recall which piece a customer liked last week.
  • • The owner can't easily see who came in today and what they looked at.

Made for retail: FurniTrail

FurniTrail runs a showroom’s daily work: GST quotations with follow-up dates, orders and advance receipts, delivery jobs closed with photos and the buyer’s sign-off, and service tickets with a deadline. Anything that slips goes to the owner.

QSR, café and cloud-kitchen chains

QSR and café chains

PlumblineAdvisory

What gets in the way

  • • Closing stock, wastage and cash are counted on paper or in WhatsApp, so gaps show up weeks later.
  • • Audit findings, short supplies and licence renewals sit in different sheets, chased by phone.
  • • Head office learns about a problem when an inspector, a franchisee or a customer raises it.
  • • New crew learn by watching, and nobody records who is cleared for which station.

Made for chains: Plumbline

Plumbline runs audits, stock variance, cash-ups, supply and dues, licences, training and guest feedback for every outlet on one sign-in. Each issue gets an owner, a deadline and proof, and anything late moves up to the area manager and head office.

Salon, spa and grooming chains

Salons and spas

ChairsideAdvisory

What gets in the way

  • • A package session can be taken off without the client in the chair.
  • • Nobody keeps a list of clients due back, so they drift to other salons.
  • • Month-end commission is worked out in Excel, and the arguments follow.
  • • The owner checks outlets through WhatsApp reports and surprise visits.

Made for salon chains: Chairside

Chairside works next to your salon POS. Each package use is confirmed by the client on their own phone, the desk works a list of clients due back, and commission is worked out only from checked services, with a morning summary per outlet for the owner.

Accounting, CA firms

Accounting and CA firms

DocketboxAdvisoryPollenRev

What gets in the way

  • • Matching purchase bills against GST returns, line by line.
  • • Typing the same figures into Tally again.
  • • Chasing clients for missing documents over WhatsApp and email.
  • • Senior people checking routine work instead of advising clients.

Made for CA firms: Docketbox

Docketbox opens a task for every client, return and period with its statutory due date, collects papers through each client’s private link, sends work for partner review, and closes a task only with the filing acknowledgement. Late work is escalated to managers and partners.

D2C, Consumer brands

D2C brands

ProforaAdvisory

What gets in the way

  • • The ad dashboard and the bank account tell different stories.
  • • Returns, shipping, payment fees and discounts sit in different places.
  • • So nobody knows the real margin left on each order.
  • • Decisions to spend more are made on numbers the team doesn't fully trust.

Made for D2C: Profora

Profora finds the products, campaigns and pincode areas that lose money after returns, COD fees, shipping and gateway charges, gives each to an owner with a deadline, and closes it only when later settled orders show the fix paid back.

Healthcare, Clinics and practices

Clinics and healthcare practices

CareCadenceAdvisory

What gets in the way

  • • The front desk types the same patient details into more than one system.
  • • Appointment reminders and follow-ups depend on someone remembering to call.
  • • Bills, claims and reports are put together by hand at the end of the month.
  • • Doctors and owners can’t easily see which days, services or doctors are busiest.

Made for clinics: CareCadence

CareCadence tracks patients due back, unpaid balances and insurer claims. The doctor sets a follow-up with one tap, the desk works one list each morning, and an item closes only on a recorded visit, payment or settlement. ClinicVitals is now part of CareCadence.

Lenders, insurance and wealth firms

Finance

CashtrailAdvisory

What gets in the way

  • • Customer documents are checked and re-typed by hand.
  • • Reports for management and regulators are rebuilt from spreadsheets every month.
  • • Follow-ups on applications and renewals depend on someone remembering.
  • • Different teams quote different numbers for the same thing.

Made for lenders: Cashtrail

Cashtrail runs each application’s statement review as a case: every balance checked, PDFs checked for signs of editing, each flag cleared with a note, and a second person signing the case off before it goes to the loan system.

Plants and workshops

Manufacturing

TaktlineAdvisory

What gets in the way

  • • Production plans live in spreadsheets that are out of date by lunch.
  • • Downtime and scrap only show up in the month-end report.
  • • Purchase, stock and sales numbers sit in different systems.
  • • Late orders get rushed at extra cost because the delay was spotted late.

Made for plants: Taktline

Taktline tracks machine stops, maintenance findings, reorders and late supplier orders, each with an owner, a deadline and proof, using Tally as the stock record. StockCompass is now part of Taktline, and distributors can take its Stock plan alone.

Transport, warehousing and distribution

Logistics and supply chain

FreightProofAdvisory

What gets in the way

  • • Shipment status is chased by phone and email.
  • • Delays are found out after the customer complains.
  • • Stock, orders and deliveries don’t match across systems.
  • • The real cost of moving each order isn’t clear.

Made for shippers: FreightProof

FreightProof checks each transporter bill against the LR and the rate card in force, opens a claim for every overcharge with its evidence, and holds the amount back from payment until a credit note or set-off is recorded.

How we pick the next sector

We add sectors one at a time, not all at once. Before we start on one, we look for a few things:

  • • Many similar businesses that run into the same problem every day.
  • • A problem that costs them time or sales, not just a nice-to-have.
  • • Owners who are willing to talk to us and try early versions.
  • • Something we can build that works without a big IT project.
