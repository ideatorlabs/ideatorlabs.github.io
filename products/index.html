Products

Software for small and mid-sized businesses in India, one product for each sector we work in. Each page covers who the product is for, what it does and what a demo includes.

All products, by sector

Retail

FurniTrail

Showroom operations for furniture stores: quotations, follow-ups, advances, delivery jobs and service tickets, each with an owner, a deadline, proof and escalation.

See how FurniTrail works → Book a demo
QSR and café chains

Plumbline

Operations suite for QSR and café chains: audits and fixes, stock variance, cash and settlements, supply and dues, licences, training and guest feedback, each issue with an owner and proof.

See how Plumbline works → Book a demo
Salons and spas

Chairside

For salon and spa chains: package uses confirmed by the client, a due-back list for the desk, commission from checked services, and a morning summary per outlet.

See how Chairside works → Book a demo
B2B companies and agencies

PollenRev

Sales software for small and mid-sized businesses: lead finding and scoring, outreach and proposals, and a deal pipeline.

See how PollenRev works → Book a demo
Accounting and CA firms

Docketbox

Compliance task tracker for CA firms: a task per client, return and period, statutory due dates, papers by link, partner approval, filing proof and escalation.

See how Docketbox works → Book a demo
D2C brands

Profora

Profit leak tracking for D2C brands: loss-making products, campaigns and COD pincode areas, each with an owner, a deadline, proof of the fix and a measured payback.

See how Profora works → Book a demo
Clinics and healthcare practices

CareCadence

Patient follow-up, unpaid balances and insurer claims for private clinics, each tracked with an owner, a due date and proof until a visit, payment or settlement closes it.

See how CareCadence works → Book a demo
Finance

Cashtrail

Statement review for NBFC and DSA credit teams: balance and tamper checks, flags cleared with notes, two-person sign-off and a report for the loan system.

See how Cashtrail works → Book a demo
Manufacturing

Taktline

Plant operations for manufacturers on Tally: machine stops, maintenance findings, reorders and late deliveries tracked to a verified close, with escalation to the owner.

See how Taktline works → Book a demo
Logistics and supply chain

FreightProof

Transporter bills checked against LRs and rate cards; each overcharge becomes a claim with an owner, a deadline and evidence, held back from payment until recovered.

See how FreightProof works → Book a demo
