Retail, for furniture showrooms

FurniTrail

For furniture showrooms

From browsing to belonging. For the buyer who says “we’ll come back with family”: they get one page in your showroom’s name that takes them from walk-in to happy home.

What it does
  • • Shortlist: the pieces they liked, with prices and sizes, to share with family
  • • Proposal: final prices and delivery, accepted on their phone
  • • Order and delivery: their page updates as the order moves
  • • Home: a thank-you that asks for a review, and a page to send a friend
  • • Every morning, your team’s Today list shows who needs them

Sales & marketing

PollenRev

For sales teams

Sales software for small and mid-sized businesses. It finds leads, writes the first message and keeps every deal in one pipeline, so a small team can sell without a big sales department.

What it does
  • • Finds leads on Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, Google search, Instagram and LinkedIn
  • • Scores each lead against your ideal customer
  • • Writes outreach, proposals and pitch decks in your brand’s voice
  • • Tracks each deal in a sales pipeline
