Furniture retail · Our first product

FurniTrail

Building · not launched yet

Software for furniture showrooms. Many people walk in, look around and leave to think it over. FurniTrail helps you keep in touch with them until they decide.

What it does
  • • Records each walk-in visitor and what they looked at
  • • Sends each visitor a shortlist link with 3 pieces
  • • Lets them view those pieces in 3D from home
  • • Follows up with them on WhatsApp
  • • Gives the owner a short summary every day

Sales & marketing

PollenRev

Private beta · we use it ourselves

Sales software for small and mid-sized businesses. It finds leads, writes the first message and keeps every deal in one pipeline, so a small team can sell without a big sales department.

What it does
  • • Finds leads on Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, Google search, Instagram and LinkedIn
  • • Scores each lead against your ideal customer
  • • Writes outreach, proposals and pitch decks in your brand’s voice
  • • Tracks each deal in a sales pipeline

Research · open concept

An open idea for a data engine that looks after itself. You say what you need from your data, and the engine works through five stages to deliver it, fix what breaks and get better over time. It is a concept we are studying, not a product you can use or buy.
