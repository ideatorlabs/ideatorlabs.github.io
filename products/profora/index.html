See what each order really left you

Profora shows D2C brands their true profit. Upload the exports you already have from Shopify, your courier and your ad accounts, and see the profit on every order after RTO, COD fees, shipping, packaging, gateway fees and GST, next to the ROAS your ads must report to pay for themselves.

Who it’s for

Brands that sell on their own store

  • • Brands on Shopify with real Meta and Google ad spend
  • • Brands with a good share of cash-on-delivery orders
  • • Founders and growth heads who still watch the numbers
The problem

If the ads look good but the bank doesn’t

  • • Meta and Google report a healthy ROAS, but little money is left.
  • • COD orders count as sales on day one, then some come back undelivered.
  • • Each return costs shipping both ways, packaging and fees, and nobody adds it up.
How it works

5 steps, from your files to what to do

  1. 1

    Upload your exports

    Shopify orders, a courier report, Meta and Google ad spend and your product costs, as CSV or Excel.

  2. 2

    Match every order

    Each order is matched to what the courier did with it. Anything that can’t be matched is listed with the reason.

  3. 3

    See true profit

    Profit per order, product, campaign and pincode, counted once the courier has delivered, returned or lost the order.

  4. 4

    Know your break-even

    The ROAS your ads must report to pay for themselves, next to the ROAS Meta and Google report.

  5. 5

    Read the week

    What changed, why it changed and what to do next, with the rows behind every sentence.

Your data

Your files aren’t kept

Files are read in memory to build the report, then dropped. There’s no database behind it, and buyer details become a one-way key used only to tell first orders from repeat buyers.
