## Who it is for: Clinics that depend on patients coming back

- Dental, dermatology, physiotherapy and small multi-specialty clinics with one to five doctors
- Front desks that recall patients from a register and the phone
- Owners who want to see what came back, what is owed and what slipped

## The problem: Follow-ups, balances and claims depend on memory

- Patients told to come back are rarely called on the right day, and many never return.
- Part-paid balances on staged treatments wait for the patient's next visit, and nobody chases them.
- Insurer deductions and rejections are rarely tracked by reason, so the same losses repeat.

## How CareCadence works

1. **The doctor sets the follow-up.** One tap at the end of a consultation: 7, 14, 30, 90 or 180 days. Recall rules per service add check-ups and next stages.
2. **The desk works one list.** Each morning: anything past its date first, then patients due, unpaid balances and claims by age, with a prepared WhatsApp message to send from the clinic's phone.
3. **Outcomes are recorded.** Contacted, booked, promised to pay, declined, or on hold while a bill is disputed or an insurer query is open.
4. **Records close the loop.** A recorded visit, payment or insurer settlement closes the item, with the record as proof. Settlements note each deduction and its reason.
5. **The owner sees what came back.** Money recovered this week from closed items only, what slipped and with whom, money pending by age, and why insurers paid less. Items past their date are escalated to the owner.

## How your data is handled

Patients, visits, bills, payments and claims are stored for each clinic separately, and no clinic can see another's. Every change to a follow-up, balance or claim is kept in an append-only log that is checked for tampering. Messages go only to patients whose recorded consent covers them, and opting out stops everything. Item records and owner emails never carry a patient's name. It is not a patient record system: no diagnoses, prescriptions or clinical notes.
