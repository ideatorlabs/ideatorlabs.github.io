# PollenRev by Ideator Labs

It finds leads on Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, Google search, Instagram and LinkedIn, scores them against your ideal customer, writes outreach, proposals and pitch decks in your brand’s voice, and tracks each deal in a sales pipeline.
