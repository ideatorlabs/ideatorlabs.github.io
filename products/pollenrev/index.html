PollenRev
PollenRev by Ideator Labs, Sales & marketing

Find leads, reach out and close deals in one place

PollenRev is sales software for small and mid-sized businesses. It finds leads, scores them against your ideal customer, writes outreach, proposals and pitch decks in your brand’s voice, and tracks each deal in a sales pipeline.

Private beta, in use by our team

Join the beta list → How it works
Who it's for

Small teams that have to sell

  • • Local businesses selling to other local businesses
  • • B2B companies with a small sales team, or none
  • • Agencies that find clients for themselves or for others
The problem

If you sell without a sales team, this may sound familiar

  • • Finding the right businesses to contact takes hours of searching.
  • • Every message and proposal is written from scratch.
  • • Leads sit in spreadsheets and chats, and follow-ups get missed.
How it works

Five steps, from profile to pipeline

  1. 1

    Describe your business

    You set up a business profile once: what you sell, who your ideal customer is, and how your brand sounds.

  2. 2

    Find leads

    PollenRev searches Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, Google search, Instagram and LinkedIn for businesses that match.

  3. 3

    Score them

    Each lead is scored against your ideal customer, so your team starts with the best fits.

  4. 4

    Write the outreach

    It drafts messages, proposals and pitch decks in your brand’s voice. Your team checks them and sends.

  5. 5

    Track every deal

    Each lead moves through one sales pipeline, so you can see what’s open and what needs a follow-up.
