Chairside
Salons and spas

Package, due-back and commission control for salon chains

Chairside works next to your salon POS for owner-run salon, spa and grooming chains with 3 to 15 outlets. Each package use is confirmed by the client on their own phone, clients due back get a ready WhatsApp message from the desk, and commission is worked out only from checked services. Anything left undone has an owner and a deadline, and reaches the outlet manager and then the owner.

The problem

Package use and commission rest on trust

  • • A package session can be taken off without the client in the chair.
  • • Clients drift away because nobody has a list of who is due back.
  • • Month-end commission is worked out in Excel, and the arguments follow.
How it works

How Chairside works

  1. 1

    The client confirms each package use

    The desk shows a QR code or sends a WhatsApp link; the client taps yes or no on their own phone. A refusal or no answer becomes a check for the outlet manager.

  2. 2

    The day's bills become checked lines

    The manager loads the POS sales report each day; every bill line becomes a checked service or product sale.

  3. 3

    The desk works the due-back list

    Clients due back for a service, and packages about to expire, each with a WhatsApp message ready. A client counts as back only on a checked visit.

  4. 4

    Commission comes from checked work

    Per-service rules or a monthly target with slabs. Stylists dispute a line from their phone; the owner approves each month, and the statement is frozen.

  5. 5

    The owner reads each outlet every morning

    Packages owed, unconfirmed uses, overdue due-backs, disputes, and patterns worth a look, with escalation when deadlines pass.

Your data

How your data is handled

Records are kept in the chain's own database, and each business and outlet sees only its own data; stylists never see client phone numbers. Every package sale, use, client answer and approved statement goes into an append-only, hash-chained log. Messages to clients are drafts the desk sends from its own WhatsApp; nothing is sent to clients from our side. Past clients and packages are loaded once from your POS export.
