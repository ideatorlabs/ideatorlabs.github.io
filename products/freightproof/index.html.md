Each bill is checked against the LR register and the rate card in force on the LR date, and each overcharge becomes a claim with an owner, a deadline and its evidence. Open claims are held back on the pay list until the transporter issues a credit note or agrees a set-off.

## Who it is for: Shippers paying many transporters

- Manufacturers shipping full and part truckloads through several transporters
- Distributors with rate cards in Excel and bills by email
- Finance and logistics teams that decide which bill amounts to pay

## The problem: Overcharges are found late, or not recovered

- Bills are checked line by line in Excel against LRs and rate cards.
- Old rates, extra charges without proof and LRs billed twice reach payment.
- Disputed amounts are tracked by phone and email, so credit notes go missing.

## How FreightProof works

1. **Bills come in.** Transporters email bills to the company's own address. Each line is matched to its LR and the rate card version in force on the LR date.
2. **Claims open.** Each amount the contract or LR record does not support becomes a claim with its evidence, an owner and a decide-by date.
3. **Approve and send.** The logistics head approves or drops each claim with a reason, then sends one proof pack per transporter.
4. **Hold and chase.** Open claims are held back on the pay list. Steps that slip are escalated to the logistics and finance heads by day.
5. **Close on the books.** A credit note or set-off is recorded, and a second person checks it against the books before the claim closes.

## How your data is handled

Bills, LR registers, rate cards and claims are stored in your company's own workspace, visible only to your logins. Each file is kept by its SHA-256 fingerprint, and every step on a claim is written to a hash-chained log that cannot be edited. Bills are read on the server; nothing is sent to an outside AI or OCR service, and FreightProof never emails your transporters. GST and MSME points are flagged for your CA, never decided. History is loaded by file import when you start.
