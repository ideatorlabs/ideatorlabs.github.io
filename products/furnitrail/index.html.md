## Who it is for: Showrooms that run on WhatsApp and order books

- Furniture and mattress showrooms with one to three branches
- Owners who chase salespeople, crews and buyers on WhatsApp
- Stores with their own or contracted delivery and fitting crews

## The problem: Follow-ups, deliveries and complaints depend on memory

- Quotations are given at the counter and many are never followed up.
- Deliveries are planned in a WhatsApp group, and damage is reported days later with no photo.
- Complaints have no list and no deadline, and when one reaches a consumer commission the buyer's chat is often the only record.

## How FurniTrail works

1. **Capture the walk-in.** The salesperson records the buyer and the pieces they liked, and sends a private link from WhatsApp.
2. **Quote and follow up.** A GST quotation with HSN codes and tax rows. Each follow-up has a date; a missed one goes to the owner.
3. **Take the order and advance.** Numbered order and receipt vouchers, the balance due, and refund vouchers before the invoice.
4. **Deliver with proof.** The crew asks the buyer to confirm the time, photographs the pieces at loading and in place, and the buyer types their name to sign off.
5. **Close service tickets.** Each complaint has an owner and a deadline, needs a photo of the fix, and closes when the buyer confirms on their page.

## How your data is handled

Everything is stored in the store's own account, kept apart from every other store, with separate logins for the owner, salespeople and crew. Every change to a follow-up, advance, job or ticket goes into an append-only, hash-chained log with photo hashes, and no buyer name or phone number is written into it. Nothing is sent on the store's behalf: WhatsApp messages open as drafts for a person to send. The catalogue can be loaded from a CSV or Excel file.
