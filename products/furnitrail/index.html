Who it's for

Furniture showrooms and the people on the floor

The problem

If you run a showroom, this may sound familiar

  • • People walk in, like a sofa, and say they'll come back with family. Most don't, and I don't have their number.
  • • A customer calls about “the grey one from last week” and nobody knows which piece they mean.
  • • Prices, discounts and delivery dates live in chats and on paper, so buyers keep asking where their order is.
  • • After delivery, nobody asks for a review or for the friend who is setting up a home too.
How it works

From walk-in to happy home

One page per buyer, in your showroom’s name. It moves with them, step by step.

  1. 1

    Walk-in

    Your salesperson notes the buyer’s name, mobile and the pieces they liked, on any phone, in about 30 seconds.

  2. 2

    Shortlist

    The buyer gets the pieces they liked, with prices and sizes, to show their family at home. You see when it’s opened, and on how many phones.

  3. 3

    Proposal

    Final prices, discount and delivery charge. The buyer accepts on their phone by typing their name.

  4. 4

    Order

    Once the advance is in, you confirm the order. The buyer’s page shows what’s paid and what’s left.

  5. 5

    Delivery

    Being made, ready, out for delivery: the page updates as the order moves, so buyers stop asking where it is.

  6. 6

    Home, review and referral

    After delivery they get a thank-you that asks for a review, and they can send your page to a friend who is setting up a home.

Every morning: the Today list

Who opened their shortlist again, who is due a call, which proposals were accepted, which deliveries are close, who to ask for a review, and which friends were referred. For each salesperson, or the whole store.
