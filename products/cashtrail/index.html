Cashtrail
Cashtrail by Ideator Labs, Finance

A borrower’s bank statements, read on one page

Cashtrail reads bank statements for small lenders, NBFCs, co-operative lenders and loan agents. Upload a borrower’s statements as the bank’s PDF, Excel or CSV download, and get one page: income, EMIs, bounces, cash flow and red flags, with the rows behind every number.

Who it’s for

Lenders who read statements by hand

  • • Small NBFCs and co-operative lenders
  • • Microfinance and credit teams
  • • Loan agents preparing files for lenders
The problem

If each file takes an hour to read

  • • Reading months of statements per borrower takes too long.
  • • Bounced EMIs and circular transfers are easy to miss.
  • • Two people can read the same statement differently.
How it works

5 steps, from your files to what to do

  1. 1

    Upload statements

    The bank’s PDF, Excel or CSV download, one or more accounts. Layouts for SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis and Kotak, and any statement with named columns.

  2. 2

    Check the balances

    Opening plus credits minus debits must equal closing, and every row must follow from the one before, to the paisa.

  3. 3

    Sort every transaction

    Income, EMIs, bounces, cash, transfers between own accounts, loans and more, by rules you can read.

  4. 4

    Read one page

    Average monthly income and balance, EMI load, bounces, cash share and red flags, each with its rows.

  5. 5

    Download

    The summary and every sorted transaction, as CSV.

Your data

Statements aren’t kept

Statements are read in memory and dropped once the page is shown. There’s no database, and a scanned PDF is marked as not readable, never guessed. Cashtrail summarises; it never scores a borrower or makes a lending decision.
