    The Method

    VALUE OS™ — diagnosis as an experiment

    Every engagement runs one disciplined protocol. No opinions without instruments; no recommendations without a number attached.

    STAGE 01DAYS 1–5

    Baseline — observe the system

    Stakeholder interviews, systems inventory, data extraction. We establish what is actually happening — not what the org chart says should happen.

    STAGE 02DAYS 5–12

    Diagnose — form and test hypotheses

    Every suspected leak becomes a hypothesis on a MECE issue tree. Each branch gets a data test and a verdict: confirmed, refuted, or inconclusive.

    STAGE 03DAYS 10–15

    Quantify — price every finding

    Margin waterfalls, time-and-volume studies, cohort analysis. Each confirmed issue is expressed in currency per year — the value map.

    STAGE 04DAYS 12–21

    Design — sequence the fixes

    Impact-versus-effort prioritization, a 12-month roadmap, a business case per initiative — owners, dates, payback periods.

    STAGE 05ONGOING

    Drive — measure realization

    We stay to build: automation, dashboards, AI workflows — tracked against the baseline we measured, reported as cumulative value realized.
